Monocle

Copy image to clipboard

Monocle can copy the full-resolution image itself to your clipboard, so you can paste it straight into another app. Reading the image data requires permission to access the site hosting it — and since image results come from all over the web, that means access to any site.

This is optional and off by default. Without it, Copy still works — it puts the image's URL on your clipboard instead of the image. Downloading and everything else in the viewer are unaffected.

Checking…

Monocle sends nothing anywhere. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Images are fetched directly from their source only when you ask to copy one.
